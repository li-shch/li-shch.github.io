Shengchang Li

Master’s student, The University of Melbourne

I work on evolutionary computation and its combination with reinforcement learning.

I am studying for a master’s degree in artificial intelligence at the University of Melbourne. Before that I was an undergraduate at Nanjing University of Information Science & Technology, where I worked with Prof. Peilan Xu.

Research interests

Evolutionary reinforcement learning, cooperative coevolution, and behavioral diversity in evolution strategies, with applications to combinatorial optimization problems such as berth allocation and crane assignment.
